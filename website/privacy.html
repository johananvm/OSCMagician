Privacy policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

About this policy

This policy describes how the OSC Magician Android app handles information when you create and use OSC control surfaces.

The app does not require an account or include advertising or analytics SDKs. It does not automatically send your settings or control data to the developer.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your pages, controls, labels, values, OSC addresses, target IP address and port, listen port, and display settings on your device. Received OSC values and text may also be saved as part of your controls. This information stays in app storage unless you export it or your device backs it up.

Your operating system may include app data in device backups, depending on your device and backup settings. Backup providers handle those backups under their own policies.

OSC network communication

The app sends OSC messages over UDP to the IP address and port you configure and listens for incoming OSC messages on your configured port. Messages can include control addresses and values; incoming messages can update controls and labels. The app also reads and displays your device's local IPv4 addresses to help you connect other OSC software.

OSC messages are not encrypted by the app. Devices on the network may be able to observe or send messages, so use a trusted network and avoid sending sensitive information. The destination device or software handles received data according to its own practices.

Import and export

If you export your screens, the app writes a JSON file containing your pages and settings, including connection details and control values, to the location you choose. If you import a file, the app reads the selected file. Your chosen file storage or sharing provider may have its own privacy practices.

Retention and deletion

Saved app data remains on your device until you change or delete it, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. Exported files and device backups are separate and may need to be deleted separately using your file manager or backup provider's settings.

Website and third-party services

This website links to GitHub for source code, downloads, and support. GitHub and any website hosting or file storage providers may handle information such as IP addresses and request logs under their own privacy policies.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the app changes. The date at the top indicates when it was last updated.

For privacy questions, please use the OSC Magician issue tracker. Issues are public, so do not include sensitive information.
